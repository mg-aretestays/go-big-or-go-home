Dynasty League Guidelines

1. Buy-In

  • Inaugural season: $50 / year with a two year commitment ($100 upfront)
  • There will be a yearly buy-in
    • Will vary, but will not go below $50 / year
    • Everyone keeps a continuous two year commitment

2. Opt-Out

If you choose to opt-out of the league you have two options:

  • Play-out the last year of your commitment (allows us time to find a replacement)
  • Completely opt-out and forfeit your buy-in for all committed years (minimum year two; year three, if applicable, pursuant of Amendment #2)

3. Draft

  • Day: Monday, August 29th, 2022
  • Time: 6:30 pm
  • Format: Snake; 2 min / pick; 24 picks / person
  • Picks will be randomized 1 day before the draft

4. Rookie Draft

  • Drafts after inaugural year
  • Date and time to be chosen after the rookie draft
  • 3 rounds
    • Round 1 selections will be 24 hours (added 6/9/23)
    • Round 2 - 3 selections will be 12 hours (added 6/9/23)

See Amendment #1 for the current rookie draft timing.

5. Scoring

  • Half-PPR with a 0.5 reception premium for TEs
  • Everything else is standard scoring

6. Roster

  • QB: 1
  • RB: 2
  • WR: 2
  • TE: 1
  • Flex: 2
  • Superflex: 1
  • IR: 1

7. Trades

  • You can trade players & draft picks
  • Trades process in 1 day, at the latest
  • I, as the commissioner, have final veto rights
    • Really only for people who have given up (hopefully, people aren’t doing this due to the 2-yr commitment)
    • If you want a trade vetoed, reach out to me
  • Trade deadline is Week 12

8. Waivers

  • Worst team has the highest waiver priority
  • Waiver holds clear after Tuesday
  • Dropped players are on waivers for 1 day

9. Playoffs

  • 8 teams make it
  • 1 week / round
  • Championship is week 17

10. Winnings

  • 1st Place: 75%
  • 2nd Place: 16.67%
  • 3rd Place: 8.33% (Buy-In Total)
  • Inaugural season winnings
    • 1st: $450
    • 2nd: $100
    • 3rd: $50

Amendments

Amendment #1 - Rookie Draft

Added 06/09/2023; updated & effective 01/07/2026

All rookie drafts going forward will have the following characteristics:

  • Start at the conclusion of teams’ Mandatory Minicamps
  • Three rounds
    • 1st round: 24 hours / pick
    • 2nd round: 4 hours / pick
    • 3rd round: 4 hours / pick

Amendment #2 - Trading 1st Rounders

Added 07/06/2023; effective 07/06/2023

Anyone who wishes to trade their 1st rounder must be paid up through that 1st rounder’s year.

  • With the 24 month commitment, this only applies to the furthest out 1st rounder.
  • If no payment is made prior to the one day deadline for a trade, the trade will be vetoed.

Amendment #3 - Tanking Mitigation

Added 11/28/2023; effective 09/07/2024

The rookie draft pick order for non-playoff teams will be determined by full-team season-long cumulative points.
